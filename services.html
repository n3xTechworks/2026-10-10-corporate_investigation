§ 01 — Overview

What we do, and for whom

We work for boards, general counsel, investors and law firms who need independent facts before a decision, a hire or a dispute.

Most engagements begin with one of two questions: who exactly are we dealing with, and where has the money gone? Background checks and M&A due diligence answer the first; forensic accounting, asset tracing and litigation support answer the second; compliance reviews test whether your own house is in order.

§ 02 — Background checks

Executive and pre-employment background checks

Verify who you are about to appoint, promote or partner with, before the announcement rather than after it.

Credentials on a CV are the easiest part of a person to check and the part most often left unchecked. Our reviews go further: identity and right-to-work, education and employment history, directorships and beneficial interests, litigation and regulatory history, and the kind of reputation that only surfaces in informed conversation.

Every check is conducted lawfully and with the candidate’s documented consent where local law requires it. Where we find something, we report the source, the date and our confidence, and we separate confirmed fact from allegation.

Scope

  • Identity, education and employment verification, including direct confirmation with institutions and former employers
  • Corporate affiliations, directorships and conflicts of interest
  • Civil litigation, bankruptcy, sanctions and adverse-media screening in the relevant languages
  • Regulatory and professional-licence history
  • Discreet reference enquiries with informed contacts, for board and C-suite appointments
  • Typical deliverablesA written report with a one-page summary of findings, an evidence index and a source log. Verbal debrief for the hiring committee or counsel on request.
  • Typical turnaroundPre-employment screening in 3 to 7 business days; executive and board-level reviews in 2 to 4 weeks, depending on the countries involved.

§ 03 — M&A due diligence

Integrity due diligence for mergers, acquisitions and investments

Financial and legal diligence tells you what a target says it is. Ours examines who stands behind it and how they have behaved.

We act for acquirers, investors and lenders who need to understand counterparty risk before terms are fixed. The work runs alongside your legal and financial advisers and is scoped to the questions that could change price, structure or the decision to proceed.

Findings are staged. An early red-flag report can reshape the rest of the engagement, so you do not pay for depth in places where the first pass already settles the question.

Scope

  • Ultimate beneficial ownership and control, traced through intermediate holding structures
  • Background and reputation of owners, directors and key managers
  • Related-party dealings, politically exposed persons and sanctions exposure
  • Litigation, regulatory and enforcement history across relevant jurisdictions
  • Anti-bribery and third-party intermediary review
  • Verification of management claims about customers, assets and track record
  • Typical deliverablesA staged red-flag report, a full integrity report with source citations, and a decision-focused briefing for the deal team and counsel.
  • Typical turnaroundRed-flag review in 5 to 10 business days; full integrity diligence in 3 to 6 weeks, with expedited timelines scoped to the deal calendar.

§ 04 — Forensic accounting

Forensic accounting and financial reconstruction

When the numbers do not reconcile, we rebuild them from source records and tell you why.

Our forensic accountants investigate suspected fraud, misappropriation and misstatement, and quantify loss for disputes and recovery. They work from ledgers, bank records and underlying documents rather than from summaries.

Scope

  • Transaction tracing and ledger reconstruction
  • Fraud and misappropriation investigations
  • Quantification of loss and damages
  • Typical deliverablesA forensic findings report, supporting schedules and, where required, expert-ready working papers.
  • Typical turnaround2 to 8 weeks, depending on data volume and access.

§ 05 — Asset tracing & recovery

Asset tracing and recovery support

Funds move faster than proceedings. We help counsel locate assets and understand the structures that hold them.

We work with counsel on judgment enforcement, fraud recovery and matrimonial or commercial disputes. Our analysts combine registry data, shipping and property records and discreet in-country enquiries to map what is owned and by whom.

Scope

  • Beneficial ownership and structure mapping
  • Real estate, vessel, aircraft and corporate holdings
  • Support for freezing and enforcement applications
  • Typical deliverablesAn asset map with source documents, and a briefing note for counsel on enforcement options.
  • Typical turnaround3 to 8 weeks for an initial trace; ongoing monitoring by arrangement.

Litigation and dispute support

Evidence gathering and analysis for counsel in complex commercial disputes and arbitrations.

We are retained by counsel so that our work product can be protected by privilege. We locate and interview witnesses, assemble documentary evidence from public and private sources, and prepare analysis your legal team can rely on.

§ 08 — How an engagement runs

A clear process, in writing

The same four steps apply whether the matter takes a week or a quarter.

  1. 01

    Confidential scoping call

    A short conversation with a partner to define the question, the risks, the jurisdictions and the decision date. No obligation, no file opened.

  2. 02

    Engagement letter

    Scope, fees, team and privilege arrangements agreed in writing, through counsel where appropriate, before any enquiry begins.

  3. 03

    Staged investigation

    Public-record, financial and human-source work led by a named partner, with interim updates so you can redirect the effort as facts emerge.

  4. 04

    Report and debrief

    An evidenced written report with sources, followed by a debrief with you and your advisers. We say plainly what we could not establish.

§ 09 — Questions

Frequently asked

Is our first conversation confidential?

Yes. Initial calls are treated as privileged and confidential. If you prefer, we can speak with your counsel first and have them retain us, which helps protect the work product.

How is the cost of an engagement determined?

Fees are based on scope, jurisdictions and seniority of the team, and are set out in the engagement letter before work starts. Staged work lets you stop or redirect after an early red-flag review.

Do you work with our law firm or in-house counsel?

Frequently. We can be retained directly by counsel, and we routinely coordinate with legal and financial advisers on live transactions and disputes.

Are your methods lawful in every country?

We use public records, licensed data sources and consented or informed human enquiries only. Local counsel and data-protection requirements are considered at scoping, and we decline any method that would breach local law.
