Independent by design, discreet by habit

A partner-led investigations firm, built in 2009 for the questions that boards, counsel and investors would rather not get wrong.

§ 01 — Our story

Two desks, one conviction: facts first

Calder Voss opened in 2009, in the uneasy months after the financial crisis, with a former regulatory enforcement lawyer, a forensic accountant and a shared view that most bad decisions begin with an unasked question.

Both had spent years on the receiving end of failed deals and missed warnings. What they saw was rarely a lack of intelligence. It was a lack of independent verification, applied early enough to change the outcome. They set out to build a firm that would supply exactly that, and nothing else: no advocacy, no stake in the transaction, and no conclusions the evidence could not carry.

The firm remains partner-owned and deliberately modest in size. We have grown by adding specialists, such as former prosecutors, compliance officers, linguists and open-source analysts, rather than by adding layers. Every engagement is still led by a named partner, and every report still says plainly what we found, how we found it and what we could not establish.

§ 02 — Principles

Four commitments we do not trade

They are written into every engagement letter and tested on every file before it leaves the building.

01

Independence

We hold no stake in the outcome. We are paid for the work, never for a conclusion, and we decline engagements where a conflict cannot be managed in the open.

02

Discretion

Enquiries are structured to protect privilege and the people involved. Access to a file is limited to the team named in the engagement letter.

03

Evidence

Nothing is reported without corroboration from a second, independent source. Where we cannot verify a claim, the report says so and does not guess.

04

Proportionality

Scope, cost and intrusion are matched to the decision at stake. We lawfully gather what the question requires and stop there.

§ 05 — Our office and approach

A quiet room, a written scope

Our New York office is built around private meeting rooms, secure file handling and a short, controlled list of people who ever see your matter.

Clients meet us here, or we come to them. Either way, the working method is the same: define the decision the work will inform, agree scope and privilege arrangements in writing, then investigate in stages so that findings can redirect the effort rather than just confirm it.

  • Engagement letters issued before any enquiry begins
  • Retention through counsel wherever privilege is a concern
  • Staged reporting, with a debrief for you and your advisers
  • Secure, access-logged storage for every working file
