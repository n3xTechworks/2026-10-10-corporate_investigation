Due diligence is only as good as the questions asked at the start.

Start with the decision, not the data

Before any research begins, define what decision the diligence will inform. Our services are scoped around that decision, which keeps costs proportionate and findings usable.

Three practical checks

  • Confirm who ultimately owns and controls the counterparty.
  • Compare stated history with independent records.
  • Test reputation through informed, discreet sources.
