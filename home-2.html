§ 03 — Questions

Before you get in touch

Is our first conversation confidential?

Yes. Initial calls are treated as privileged and confidential. If you would rather not describe the matter directly, we can speak first with your counsel or a nominated adviser.

How long does an investigation take?

It depends on scope and the jurisdictions involved. A focused background check can be a matter of days; a cross-border diligence review usually takes several weeks. We set out a realistic timeline in the engagement letter, before work begins.

Can we engage you through our lawyers?

Frequently, and often preferably. Being retained by counsel can help support privilege over the work product, and we are used to working within a legal team's instructions and reporting lines.
