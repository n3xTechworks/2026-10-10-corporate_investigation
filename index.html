§ 03 — Confidentiality

Six commitments we make to every client

Discretion is a working practice, not a slogan. These terms go into every engagement letter.

  1. 01

    Need-to-know staffing

    Each matter is run by a small, named team. No one outside it sees your name, your file or your question.

  2. 02

    Engagement through counsel

    Where privilege matters, we can be retained by your lawyers so that work product is protected as far as the law allows.

  3. 03

    Lawful methods only

    Public records, consented enquiries and lawful sources. No pretexting, no intercepts, no access to protected systems.

  4. 04

    Source protection

    Sources are not named in a report without their agreement, and we explain how we handle them before we begin.

  5. 05

    Controlled files

    Matter files sit in access-controlled, encrypted systems with an audit trail of who has opened them.

  6. 06

    Clean close-out

    When the work ends, materials are returned or securely destroyed in line with the engagement letter and legal retention duties.
